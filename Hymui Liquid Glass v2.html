02 — Anatomy of HmLiquidSurface

Not a blurred rectangle

  • 1Luminous edge. A 1px border plus inset top highlight — the light catching a polished rim. Never blur alone.
  • 2Modular corner. One corner carries the logo's module form — a quiet Hymui signature on panels and dialogs.
  • 3Pointer highlight. A soft specular sheen glides with the cursor. Disabled on low-power devices and under reduced transparency.

08 — Accessibility contract
  • →Text on glass always meets WCAG AA — tint alpha rises automatically over busy backgrounds.
  • →Reduced transparency swaps every surface to near-solid (α 0.94, blur 10) — try the tweak on this page.
  • →No backdrop-filter support? Surfaces fall back to solid fills with the same edge light — blur is never the only separation.
  • →2px mint focus ring, 3px offset, on every interactive element — tab through this page.
  • →State is never transparency alone: running agents pair the mint glow with the outline mark and a label.
